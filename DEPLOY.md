# Deploy DebateResearcher (public URL)

Your code is on GitHub: **https://github.com/xiao1dian/debateresearcher**

## One-click deploy (free, ~5 minutes)

1. Open this link: **[Deploy on Render](https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/xiao1dian/debateresearcher)**

2. Sign in with **GitHub** (same account: `xiao1dian`).

3. On the deploy screen:
   - **Service name:** `debateresearcher` (this becomes your URL)
   - **Plan:** Free
   - Click **Apply** / **Deploy Blueprint**

4. Wait 3–5 minutes for the build to finish.

5. Your public URL will be:
   ```
   https://debateresearcher.onrender.com
   ```
   (Render shows the exact link on the dashboard when deploy completes.)

---

## After it's live

- Share that URL anywhere — it works on any phone or computer.
- To appear on **Google**, submit the URL at [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) (free). Indexing can take a few days to weeks.
